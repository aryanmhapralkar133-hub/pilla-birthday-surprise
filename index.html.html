Our Little Story ♡

Some stories are made of grand moments. Ours is made of little things I never want to forget.

Sometimes, the sweetest stories begin without us even realising it. I never knew that someone I barely noticed would slowly become someone I'd look forward to seeing. It happened so naturally, through little smiles and moments that quietly found a place in my heart.

And before I knew it, she had become someone I loved noticing. That's where my favourite story began. ❤️
The simplest conversations slowly became my favourite part of the day. From walking together to class to those little walks after class, every moment started feeling special.

And somehow, it always seemed to rain just when we met. Maybe it was just a coincidence, but those little rainy walks became some of my favourite memories.

And then there were our little bus journeys… sitting or standing together, holding your hand, and finding happiness in the simplest moments. I never knew such little things could mean so much. ❤️
A sports boy and a topper girl… quite an unexpected combination, isn't it? We were different in so many ways. I was the quiet one, while you brought life to every little moment.

I don't know exactly when or how you became my favourite person. Maybe it was your maturity, your beautiful heart, your understanding, or those little ways you showed your love without even saying it.

You taught me what it feels like to love someone truly. All I know is that nobody else ever felt quite like you. You became my first love, and the one my heart never wanted to replace. ❤️
Some feelings don't arrive all at once. They grow quietly, in the small moments, the familiar smiles, and the comfort of simply being together. Looking back, I'm grateful for every little moment that brought us closer. ♡
It was never just one thing. It was your understanding, your maturity, your smile, and the thoughtful little ways you showed that you cared. Maybe you didn't even realise I noticed them, but I did. And I still do. ❤️
There are so many things I adore about you, and some of the sweetest ones are the things you don't even notice about yourself. I could name a thousand reasons, but somehow they would all lead back to one person — you. ♡
If I could go back and live these moments again, I would still want to know you, share those little smiles, and choose every small moment that led me here. Some people are simply worth choosing, again and again. ❤️
This isn't the end of our story. There are still places to discover, memories to make, little adventures to share, and so many pages waiting to be written. Whatever the next chapters hold, I hope we keep filling them with love, laughter, and us. ❤️

And this is only the beginning…
